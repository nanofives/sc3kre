- `FUN_10003eee` **Init** `[@0x10003eee]` and `FUN_10004f1e` **Shutdown** `[@0x10004f1e]` are the layer's construct/destruct entry points. Init stores the module **singleton at `DAT_1001fdb8`**, allocates the whole grid set, and registers **view CLSID `0x29ca805`** and command **`traffic_lights` (`0x837961ea`)**. Note: this is `0x29ca805`, one below the module's own `0x029ca806` — it is the sub-view/data class id, confirmed by string-index registration, not the layer clsid.

**Per-tick pathfinding / assignment core (the step the task asked to chase).**
- `FUN_100070d0` `sc3_transit_route_trip` is the **trip-routing driver**: it seeds a cost, runs a work-queue expansion calling `FUN_1000755e` per step, and on success deposits trip volume across the origin rect via `FUN_10006edd`.
- `FUN_1000755e` `sc3_transit_select_next_cell` is the **inner cost/neighbour selector** — the actual per-cell commute cost formula.
- `FUN_100093e9` `sc3_transit_recompute_stats` is the **per-tick aggregation**: it scans the full grid and folds ordinance modifiers into the traffic statistics and the data-view.

**Tunable tables (named + magic).** These are the transit tunables:
- `DAT_1001f0b0 .. DAT_1001f0ba` — per-mode **base trip cost** (indexed by transit type 1/2/4/8/0x14/0x18/0x30/0x41/0x42/0x43/0x50) `[@0x1000755e]`.
- `DAT_1001f0c1 .. DAT_1001f0ca` — per-mode **cost divisors**; `DAT_1001f0c0` = **impassability/congestion threshold** `[@0x1000755e]`.
- `DAT_1001f0be` — **trip-volume-per-cell** multiplier `[@0x100070d0]`.
- `DAT_1001f0d0` base + `DAT_1001f0d4/d8/dc/e0/e4` **ordinance deltas**, keyed by ordinance CLSIDs `0x15b4d1e / 0xe2bf1e2a / 0xa2bf1ddc / 0xc2bf1def / 0x62f6e7cf` `[@0x100093e9]`.
