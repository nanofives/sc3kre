# BAT Slots

Put your own Building Architect buildings into your zones without losing any of the game's buildings.

The game's Building Replacement lets you swap a stock building for one you made, but then the stock one is gone.
BAT Slots adds 16 extra zoned buildings, called slots, that grow next to the stock ones. Put your building on a slot
and it grows in that zone type from then on. Every stock building keeps growing.

## How to use it

1. Install the mods package as usual. The slots are part of it.
2. Add your .bld building to the game's BAT list ("Edit list" in the replace window) if it isn't there yet.
3. In a city, find a slot building with the query tool. Its name starts with "BAT Slot", for example
   "BAT Slot Light Residential Low Wealth 1x1".
4. Click Replace in the query window, pick your building from the list, click the Replace arrow, then OK.

Every building of that slot in the city now shows your building, and new ones keep growing. The choice is saved with
the city.

## Good to know

- Your building has to be the same size as the slot. The list only shows buildings that fit. The slots come in 1x1
  and 2x2.
- A slot nobody has used looks exactly like an ordinary game building, so your cities look the same until you use one.
- A slot grows about as often as any one stock building of its zone type.
- The query window only shows Replace on finished buildings, not on ones that are under construction or abandoned.
- If you load a city without the mods, the slot buildings disappear and their lots fill up again with stock
  buildings. Nothing crashes.
